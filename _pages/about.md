I am a PhD student at [Georgia Tech](https://www.gatech.edu/), advised by [Prof. Ada Gavrilovska](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/~ada/) and [Dr. Ketan Bhardwaj](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/~ketanbj/).

My research builds systems and networking solutions for Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite networks. I have worked across control plane management, edge application deployment, route variability, cellular deployment, and storage, as well as the use of LEO networks as a national emergency failover and orchestration for space-based solar power.

Before Georgia Tech, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering with Honors at [IIT Bombay](https://www.iitb.ac.in/), and I have since spent time at Microsoft Research, MediaTek, Microsoft, and IBM.
